## Selected projects

### 1. IBM — Global Training Operations and Fulfillment Automation

**Challenge.** Instructor-led training required coordination of classes, worldwide instructor availability, enrollments, and fulfillment activities as program volume increased.

**My work.** Designed and developed applications to coordinate instructor availability and assignments. Built fulfillment databases supporting education enrollments and fulfillment, with integrations to learning management and other business systems. Automated routine notifications and fulfillment steps.

**Impact.** Developed applications that supported the growth of education enrollments from approximately **200 to more than 1,200 per month**. The instructor scheduling solution helped coordinate availability and assignments for **more than 600 instructors worldwide**, reducing the need for manual coordination.

**Capabilities demonstrated:** Workflow and scheduling design, database applications, system integrations, notifications, requirements analysis.

### 2. Lennar — Enterprise Records and eDiscovery Process Automation

**Challenge.** Locating and processing enterprise records for eDiscovery requests involved substantial manual effort.

**My work.** Developed a **Lotus Notes-based eDiscovery application** to streamline the identification and processing of relevant records.

**Impact.** Reduced processing time **from weeks to minutes** by automating previously manual eDiscovery activities. The solution generated approximately **$300,000 in estimated business savings**.

**Capabilities demonstrated:** Process analysis, enterprise application development, information-retrieval workflows, efficiency improvements.

### 3. Lennar — Construction Vendor Progress Report Automation

**Challenge.** Vendors submitted construction completion and progress reports by email. Teams needed reports filed in the correct project locations without repetitive manual sorting.

**My work.** Developed an automated workflow that processed incoming vendor progress emails and placed the reports in the appropriate files for construction personnel.

**Impact.** Eliminated repetitive manual filing of incoming vendor reports, improved document organization, and made construction progress information easier for teams to access.

**Capabilities demonstrated:** Email-driven automation, document routing, business rules, collaboration workflows.
